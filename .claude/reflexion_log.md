## S170 (2026-09-09) — audited S169's compacted handoff (12 agents, 7 false claims found), shipped 6 priorities, and rebuilt fog of war FOUR times before getting it right

- #one-populations-ceiling-silently-gating-another: GOBLIN_MAX_PER_SPAWNER=10 was shared by the goblin tower and the tier-3 race towers, so a tier-3 tower went permanently silent at ten units - the owner's original 'the Piranha didn't produce at all' returning, months later, from a constant named after a different unit. - #the-unit-of-a-ruling-is-part-of-the-ruling: the sudoku trial counted SHAPES with an exact test; the owner had said 'twelve same-shape-connectors' and the code's own comment flagged that one word would flip it - and nobody asked him for two sessions while he reported 'there is no sudoku, it doesn't work'. LESSON: a visual for a property of an ENTITY belongs in a module, not in whichever renderer happened to add it. - #research-the-standard-solution-before-inventing-a-mechanism: I spent most of a session and THREE shipped-and-wrong attempts hiding enemy units, by re-layering a backdrop twice and then building an inverse Pixi mask - without ever checking how fog of war is done. LESSON: when a problem is old and solved everywhere, the first move is to find the standard mechanism, not to reach for a rendering trick. SECOND, SHARPER LESSON: prefer the mechanism the SUITE CAN SEE. All three failures passed every gate, because masks and compositing are invisible to a test harness that cannot read composed frames - renderer.extract does not apply filter effects. A pure predicate was both the correct answer and the testable one, and "can I assert this?" would have picked it on day one.
