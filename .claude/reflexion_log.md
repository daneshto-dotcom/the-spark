## S170 (2026-09-09) — audited S169's compacted handoff (12 agents, 7 false claims found), shipped 6 priorities, and rebuilt fog of war FOUR times before getting it right

- #a-green-test-can-be-defending-the-bug: the owner reported the same two-pharaoh defect twice and S169 'fixed' it by removing the gate that was PROVABLE in a unit test (a direct-dispatch latch) while leaving the gate he actually hit. t9BossTower.test.ts asserted the waiting behaviour as CORRECT, so the suite was green ON the defect and would have blocked the real fix. LESSON: when a reported bug 'does not reproduce' in tests but the owner keeps reporting it, grep for a test that ASSERTS the reported behaviour - a defect with a passing test defending it is invisible to every gate. Also: the docblock defending that gate justified itself by citing the latch S169 had just deleted, so a stale rationale outlived its own premise.

- #gate-the-effect-on-the-outcome-not-on-the-trigger: the drone's blast VFX was pushed unconditionally, BEFORE the enemy-only candidate scan and the owner-sparing damage - so a fuse-expiry detonation at home showed a full shock ring for an event that could not damage anything. The tempting fix (silence blasts on fuse expiry) would have silenced real hits too, since explode-in-place next to an enemy base is deliberate. LESSON: gate a visual on what actually HAPPENED (candidates.length), never on the branch that led there.

- #one-populations-ceiling-silently-gating-another: GOBLIN_MAX_PER_SPAWNER=10 was shared by the goblin tower and the tier-3 race towers, so a tier-3 tower went permanently silent at ten units - the owner's original 'the Piranha didn't produce at all' returning, months later, from a constant named after a different unit. Three prior exclusions in the same function existed for exactly this failure and a fourth was still owed. LESSON: fix it as an EXEMPTION, never by raising or deleting the shared number, and assert BOTH sides - a test proving 'tier 3 is uncapped' cannot tell that from 'the cap is gone' and would have re-tuned an economy the owner chose.

- #the-unit-of-a-ruling-is-part-of-the-ruling: the sudoku trial counted SHAPES with an exact test; the owner had said 'twelve same-shape-connectors' and the code's own comment flagged that one word would flip it - and nobody asked him for two sessions while he reported 'there is no sudoku, it doesn't work'. Twelve connectors is not a fixed shape count (chain of 13 = ring of 12 = 12 bonds), so no shape-count test could express his rule. LESSON: when a comment says 'one word flips this if the intent was X', that is an OWNER QUESTION, not a note. And an exact-N test on a value that can jump by two is skippable by construction.

- #a-private-renderer-method-scopes-a-condition-to-one-renderer: the stun stars lived as a private method of goblinRenderer, which silently limited them to GOBLIN_KINDS - while applyStun's only caller, the Kraken sonar, has NO type filter and stuns voltkin/chewer/drones drawn by two other renderers. Those froze with no stars, reading as a hang. The same method also used flat geometry, so on the scaled-up bosses (the units stun exists to counter) the ring sat inside the sprite. LESSON: a visual for a property of an ENTITY belongs in a module, not in whichever renderer happened to add it. Extraction is also what made it testable - it went from an untestable private method to a pure function with a determinism assertion.

- #research-the-standard-solution-before-inventing-a-mechanism: I spent most of a session and THREE shipped-and-wrong attempts hiding enemy units, by re-layering a backdrop twice and then building an inverse Pixi mask - without ever checking how fog of war is done. The owner had to ask twice ("did you look into them or no? I said, look at Red Alert") before I looked. The classic RTS answer is per-entity culling (dont draw it), the repo ALREADY contained two of the three pieces (explored grid + last-seen ghosts), and the fix was one predicate plus one line per renderer. LESSON: when a problem is old and solved everywhere, the first move is to find the standard mechanism, not to reach for a rendering trick. SECOND, SHARPER LESSON: prefer the mechanism the SUITE CAN SEE. All three failures passed every gate, because masks and compositing are invisible to a test harness that cannot read composed frames - renderer.extract does not apply filter effects. A pure predicate was both the correct answer and the testable one, and "can I assert this?" would have picked it on day one.
