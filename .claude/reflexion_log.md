- #the-forcing-function-fired-on-cue-and-its-eight-siblings-stayed-silent — Adding a hashed World scalar is NINE sites. - #the-verifier-op-table-has-ge-not-gte-and-the-difference-is-SILENT — An MCV grep_count binding with op "gte" reported FAIL on a condition that was plainly true (count=10, value=8). _OPS in verify-session-claims.py holds eq/ne/gt/ge/lt/le - "gte" is simply absent, and the lookup falls through to the "==" DEFAULT rather than erroring.
