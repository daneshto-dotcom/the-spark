⛔ **"+10% HP to all spawned units" CANNOT BE EXPRESSED, AND IT IS HIS OWN R125 THAT MAKES IT SO.**

The castle-spawned unit is **1 HP / 1 DEF / 1 ATK / 1 PEN** — `RACE_UNIT_HP`…`RACE_UNIT_PEN`,
`constants.ts:1733-1736`, docblocked *"R125, VERBATIM … Four owner numbers, not mine."* On the one
ladder that is:

```
pool   = 1 × (5 + 1) =  6 fifths
damage = 1 × (5 + 1) =  6 fifths
```

**10% of 6 is 0.6.** `damageEntity` throws on a non-integer amount by design — the whole stat system
is integer fifths precisely so no float can reach the damage path and diverge host from the
`?worker=1` mirror. So a 10% buff either truncates to **0** (the buff does nothing at all) or rounds
to **1** (a +16.7% buff wearing a "10%" label).

This is the same wall §9.4 of `SPARK_RACES_SPEC.md` hit in S162, which is why **R105** exists
("a zombie revives at 1 HP flat, not at 10% of max") and why **R118** ruled the draft grants
**+1 POINT** on an axis rather than a percentage.

**WHAT I SHIPPED, AND WHY.** `+1 POINT` on the named axis, the R118 shape — the only integer-safe
option — with the magnitude behind **one constant** he can retune (`DRAFT_POINTS_PER_PICK`).
Measured effect on the unit he will actually see:

| pick | race unit before | after | change |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **+1 HP** | pool 6 | pool **12** | +100% |
| **+1 DEF** | pool 6 | pool **7** | +16.7% |
| **+1 ATK** | hit 6 | hit **12** | +100% |
| **+1 PEN** | hit 6 | hit **7** | +16.7% |

⚠ **THE TWO AXES ARE NOT THE SAME SIZE, AND THAT IS THE LADDER'S DOING, NOT A BUG.** HP and ATK are
integer COUNTS on a 1-based unit, so the smallest step is a doubling. DEF and PEN are the
`1 + 0.2n` multiplier, so the smallest step is +16.7% at DEF 1. §9.8 claims "+1 DEF = exactly +20%";
that is true only at DEF **0**, and the race unit sits at DEF 1. ⭐ Corrected in canon by this
session rather than left to mislead the next one.

**THE ONE-LINE LEVER IF HE WANTS FINER STEPS**, and §9.8 already named it: raise the race unit's BASE
HP and ATK so there is room for smaller relative steps. That is a balance change he has not asked
for, so it is reported, not taken.

## 4 · DESIGN

### P1 — the substrate

`world.draft: DraftEvent | null`, beside `world.sudoku`, minted in `applyStartGame` and at each
qualifying BUILD edge in `hostTick`.

```
DraftEvent = {
  openedAtTick: number        // relative-deadline base, the tickSudoku form
  waveNumber:   number        // which draft this is (0 = the pre-wave-1 one)
  picks:        Map<PlayerId, DraftOption>   // resolved picks only
  botPicks:     Map<PlayerId, DraftOption>   // decided AT OPEN (see 3.3)
}
```

- **Opens** at `applyStartGame` (the level-0 draft) and on entry into BUILD when
  `(waveNumber - 1) % DRAFT_WAVE_INTERVAL === 0`.
- **Deadline** is the remaining BUILD, checked relative: `world.tick - openedAtTick >= …`.
- ⛔ **IT DOES NOT FREEZE THE SIM.** R106 says the draft never blocks the sim, and he confirmed the
  behaviour himself — *"they can take their time, they can take like as much as the whole build
  phase"*. The BUILD continues underneath; only the panel is up. This is the one place the NONET
  template is deliberately **not** copied, and the reason is recorded at the constant.
- **No-choice** auto-takes **the racial one** — his S187 ruling, which **reverses R106**
  (auto-general). ⚠ Recorded as a reversal, not silently applied. **And because every racial is
  COMING SOON today, the auto-pick falls back to the general option** whenever no racial is
  offered — otherwise the deadline would grant nothing at all.

### P2 — the general track

Axis by draft index, cycling: **HP → DEF → ATK → PEN → HP → …** So L0 = HP, L5 = DEF, L10 = ATK,
L15 = PEN, L20 = HP. ⚠ The **cycle** is mine; he gave the order ("health and then defense and then
attack and penetration") and the wave points, not what happens after PEN. A cycle never runs out,
which R101's "recurring with no ceiling" requires.

Stored per seat as four small integers on `Player` (the `castleRegenLevel` precedent), applied at
creature birth in **both** seed sites (§3.4).
