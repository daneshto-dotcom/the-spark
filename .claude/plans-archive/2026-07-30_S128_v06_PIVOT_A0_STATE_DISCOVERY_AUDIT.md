### B3 · The economy cannot run at the shipped faucet — and the faucet is absent from all four docs

### B4 · Directives + a bank of 8-10 delete the carve-down tactic — the one piece of evidence the rebuild is justified by
`V06_DESIGN.md:41-43` makes carve-down-to-a-recipe the emergent behaviour the whole pivot exists to protect and accelerate. Verified recipe sizes — every one is an **exact, isolated component**: pentagram = exactly 5 Triangles in a closed 5-cycle, "the component contains NO other primitives" (`src/state/godlyRecipes/pentagram.ts:4-6`); lightningHub = 1 Dot + 5 Circles = 6 (`lightningHub.ts:3-6`); princessHelga = 7 (`princessHelga.ts:4`); voltkin = exactly 8 (`voltkin.ts:4`); laserTurret = exactly 8 (`laserTurret.ts:4`).

Six isolated 5-Triangle rings score identically to one 30-prim structure with the same bonds.

⇒ Carving was never economically motivated. It was *forced* by uniform-random types + carry-1. A hard type filter plus a buffer of 8-10 (≥ every recipe's exact size) makes the rational play "assemble the exact recipe directly, off to one side, first try." Nothing left to carve. The pivot removes the cause of the behaviour it promises to move to minute one — and this is exactly what the S135 gate asks.
**Owner ruling required before S131/S132.** Options: (a) directives **bias** the collect mix (e.g. 2:1) instead of hard-filtering; (b) bank cap strictly **below 5** (pentagram is the floor) so no recipe is assemblable from one bank-load; (c) accept the loss and rewrite the S135 criterion. Recommend (a)+(b): bank 4, biasing directive. Put the recipe-size table in the Blueprint next to the bank-cap number so the two are never tuned independently again.

### B5 · Score is quadratic in time; nothing in the roadmap owns retuning the win score
`src/state/scoring.ts:245` `perTickFactor = SCORE_INCOME_PER_COMPLEXITY_PER_SEC / PHYSICS_HZ`, `:257` `next = prev + complexity · perTickFactor`; `constants.ts:265` = 0.05, `:308` = 1500. With complexity growing linearly, score(T) = 0.0125·T² ⇒ match length ∝ 1/√throughput. Automate haul at 3× material flow → win at **~200 s**; at the 5× the §VII.6 upgrades explicitly target → **~155 s**. The bank cap does not save this: it bounds *hoarding*, not *flow*.

The docs never state a target match length and no session in S126-S150 owns `PHASE_1_WIN_SCORE` or `SCORE_INCOME_PER_COMPLEXITY_PER_SEC` — S135's balance pass lists "worker count, cycle time, bank cap and upgrade costs" (`V06_DESIGN.md:417`) and omits both. Meanwhile §1 condemns the 50→150→210→630→786→1500 climb as "six attempts to patch a single unresolved problem"; the pivot forces a seventh, larger, unowned raise. **Must be ruled together with B3** — raising supply and holding match length are one decision.
