SPARK Team Backdrops

Gemini prompts for the 2v2 team-half backdrops. Team one gets the left half and team two the right, and each half is the same area one race covers in a 1v1 today. You need 15 images for the mixed-race pairs. The 6 same-race teams reuse the race's existing 1v1 backdrop, and their prompts are at the bottom as optional extras. Every prompt follows the S165 originals: the same wording, the same six race worlds, the same model family.

Technical spec

Region one image covers
Exactly the region one race covers in a 1v1 (PITCH_2P) match today: one HALF of the 1920 x 1080 board, 960 x 1080 world px. Team one = left half (x 0-960), team two = right half (x 960-1920). Top seat = y 0-540, bottom seat = y 540-1080 (QUADRANTS_4P zones 0+3 on the left, 1+2 on the right).
Shipped file
480 x 540 px, 8:9, PNG, RGB, no alpha - identical to public/art/race-zones/zone-<race>-2p.png. Drawn cover-scaled at 2x, at 0.55 alpha, behind everything.
What to ask Gemini for
Portrait 4:5 (Nano Banana Pro gives ~928 x 1152). Fallback 3:4 (~896 x 1200) - the ratio the six originals were generated at. Never square, never landscape.
Crop + resize (merge owner)
Cover-scale to 960 x 1080, centre-crop, then LANCZOS-resize to 480 x 540. 4:5 loses ~4.7% top and bottom; 3:4 loses ~8.6%. The same two steps S165 applied to the originals.
File orientation
Generate every image as the LEFT half: the RIGHT edge is the centre line, kept calm. Alphabetically-first race on TOP. The renderer flips X for team two and flips Y when the alphabetically-later race holds the top seat.
Keep plain
Top-left and bottom-left corners (castles at (130,130) and (130,950), 121 px keep-out); right edge at mid-height (quarry disc r = 127 at (960,540) is punched out of the texture); bottom 84 px (footer bar, y 996-1080); right-edge band (centre line, the other team's wall).
Brightness gate
The originals measured mean max-channel 22-41 / 255 with zero pixels above 200. Run the check in the .md before shipping.
Where files go
You: download full size and send the path. Master: assets-source/race-zones/team/zone-team-<a>-<b>.png. Shipped: public/art/race-zones/zone-team-<a>-<b>.png (a, b alphabetical).

One file is the LEFT half of the board. The right edge is the centre line and stays empty. The quarry hole, half a disc at mid-height, is cut out in code. The castles sit in the two left corners. The footer bar covers the bottom strip. The two races meet in a soft band across the middle. Team two's half uses the same file mirrored left-to-right. If the races are the other way round, top and bottom, the code mirrors it top-to-bottom. That only works because the camera looks straight down and there's no sky, so the prompts insist on it.

How to generate in Gemini

Steps

  1. Open gemini.google.com, start a new chat for every image (old chats leak the previous scene), and pick the image tool with the Pro / Thinking model (Nano Banana Pro). That is the same model family the six originals came from (gemini-3-pro-image); the fast Nano Banana model is weaker at layout instructions.
  2. Optional but recommended: attach the two races' existing masters (paths on each card) as style references and add one line: "Use the attached images ONLY for palette, materials and mood - not for their camera angle." This is how the originals were kept as a family (the 2p set was seeded off the 4p set).
  3. Paste the prompt. If the result is not portrait, reply: "Same image, but as a tall portrait 4:5 frame."
  4. Generate 2-4 tries per pair and keep the best one. Download at full size (the download button, not a screenshot).
  5. Send the file path to the session. It does the crop, resize, flip wiring, brightness check and commit.

Reject the image if

  • Any sky, horizon, moon or side-on view (it cannot be flipped and will not match the board).
  • Bright or white patches, neon colour, or a busy, high-contrast picture.
  • Any text, runes that read as letters, a watermark, a frame or a vignette.
  • Characters, creatures, skeletons or standing buildings.
  • A hard straight line, wall, river or road across the middle, or a two-panel look.
  • Landmarks against the right edge, in the left corners, or in the bottom strip.
  • Anything that looks like a known game, film or franchise.
  • Landscape or square output.

Shared avoid block (already appended to every prompt)

Avoid: sky, horizon, moon, sun, clouds, mountains seen from the side, any perspective, side or isometric view; characters, people, creatures, animals, monsters, skeletons, vehicles, units; standing buildings or castles seen from the side; text, letters, numbers, runes that read as writing, logos, signature, watermark, border, frame, vignette, UI, icons, grid, hex tiles, map markers; bright highlights, white or near-white areas, neon saturation, high contrast, a sharp focal point, busy fine detail; a straight dividing line, a wall, a road or a river splitting the two halves, a split-screen or two-panel look, mirrored or symmetrical halves; anything in the right-hand band of the image or in the top-left and bottom-left corners; the recognisable style, characters or locations of any existing game, film or franchise.
