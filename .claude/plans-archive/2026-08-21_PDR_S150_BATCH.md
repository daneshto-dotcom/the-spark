### ⭐ DELTA 1 — I WAS WRONG ABOUT "BOTS BUILD TOWERS". It is Full tier; the prior session's call was right.

I initially sized it Standard: `BUILD_BLUEPRINT` is one already-allowlisted client intent,
`planBlueprintPayment` is a pure affordability oracle, `stampRefusalAt` already enforces phase +
zone + overlap, and `BotGoal` has no wire surface. All of that is true and verified. **It is also the
small half.** What refutes my sizing:

> **STRUCTURAL PROOF that a bot can never build a tower — sharper than "no recipe goal exists".**
> `GROWTH_STEP = AUTO_BOND_RADIUS * 0.8` = **48**, which is INSIDE `AUTO_BOND_RADIUS = 60`. So every
> primitive a bot places auto-bonds into its ONE existing component, which grows **monotonically** —
> while all six recipes gate on an **EXACT** component size (4/5/6/7/7/8) with strict `!==` and no
> upper tolerance. Past the eighth shape, ignition is **mathematically impossible**.
> Verified: `botBrain.ts:34-36`, `constants.ts:721`, `laserTurret.ts` ("strict `!==` with NO upper
> tolerance").

Worse: `smartPlacement` (HARD + IMBA) **actively weaves redundancy bonds**, precisely what the
blueprint stamper documents itself as avoiding because it kills exact-degree recipes. And a shipped
test **pins the blob the owner is complaining about** as correct (`botGameplay.test.ts:185-210`).

Plus **self-sabotage**: every primitive a bot has ever placed forbids a 60px clearance disc for any
stamp (`blueprintLegality.ts:106-112`), and voltkin needs ~212px clear radius inside a 960×540
quadrant. Adding only the goal produces bots that pick a tower and find **nowhere legal to put it** —
which reads to the owner as the same bug, not a fix.

**The cost is design, not lines:** three new pure functions with no precedent in the repo (ranking
policy, stamp-site search, shape-shopping planner), the first **goal persistence** the bot
architecture has ever had (BotController holds five fields and re-decides from scratch every think),
a new `BotConfig` axis across four difficulty rows, and blast radius into shipped tests.
**Full tier. Still NO protocol bump** (host-only bots, no wire surface, `BUILD_BLUEPRINT` allowlisted
since v21).

Both external seats argued the rng-stream shift is the gating risk; **Gemini went further and said it
forces a protocol bump.** Both are wrong, and the truth is worse than either:

All **three** determinism gates are **self-comparing** (run vs re-run, new vs frozen reference), not
golden-hash pins — `botController.test.ts:121-144`, `hostTick.replay.test.ts`,
`hostTick.differential.test.ts`. A new bot goal changes behaviour for a given seed and **breaks none
of them**. The change lands **silently**.

**REFUTED — Gemini, "bot behaviour changes require a protocol bump."** The repo's criterion is stated
verbatim on `HelloMsg`: *"bumped on wire-incompatible changes"*, and every changelog entry justifies
itself with a **peer-disagreement** story. S133 explicitly did **not** bump for additive-optional
fields. Bots are host-only (`hostTick.ts:629-631`); a client receives their effects via NetSnapshot
and never simulates them, so two peers on the same build cannot disagree. **The mitigation is a TEST,
in this repo's established idiom** (`spawnerRngInvariance.test.ts` exists for exactly this class of
risk), not a version bump.
