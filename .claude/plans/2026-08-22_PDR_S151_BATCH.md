## 5. ⭐ OWNER RULING R75 — TOWERS HAVE NO HP OR DEF. THE CONNECTORS DO.

> *"towers have attack and piercing but not def and hp because they are based on the connectors that
> build them. its the connectors that have different hp and def (think about it)"*

**This DISSOLVES the §5 question rather than answering it, and it dissolves both Council objections with it.**
Gemini's fractional-DoT-on-a-12-HP-tower hazard cannot arise — a tower has no HP pool. Grok's
"3000→12 destroys tuning granularity" cannot arise for the same reason. Neither seat considered this.

### The stat model is now THREE families, each taking the subset that matches what it physically is

| Family | HP | DEF | ATK | PEN | Rationale |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|---|
| **Units** (creatures) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | Full combatants |
| **Towers** (defenders) | — | — | ✓ | ✓ | Durability is EMERGENT from the connectors they are built of |
| **Connectors** (bonds) | ✓ | ✓ | — | — | They *are* the structure's body |

### Four pieces of evidence that the codebase already leans this way (probed, not argued)

1. **`Bond` has no `hp` field.** `BondHashed` = `id·aId·bId·restLength·stiffnessTier·createdTick·stiffnessMultiplier·a·b`. The slot the owner wants is genuinely empty.
2. **A connector's identity is ALREADY per-shape-pair.** `combos.ts` assigns a `stiffnessTier` to each of 36 combo entries (14 magic names, 3 tiers), and `SerializedBond` **already carries `stiffnessTier` on the wire**. If connector HP/DEF derive from the combo or tier, that half of the stat system costs **ZERO new wire fields**. `ComboOutcome.areaMultiplier` is existing precedent for a reserved per-combo stat slot.
3. **Tower HP was an unvalidated bolt-on, and constants.ts says so in its own words:** *"⚠ FIRST-PASS BALANCE. Nothing in the game deals damage to a defender yet, so these numbers are unvalidated by play."* `TURRET_DEFENDER_MAX_HP`/`PRINCESS_DEFENDER_MAX_HP` replaced a `DEFENDER_HP = 1e9` sentinel whose entire meaning was *"defenders die by recipe-break, not damage."* **R75 reverts to the original design intent.**
4. **`chewHits` is the same defect class as the goblin backbone.** A connector's durability lives on the ATTACKER (`CREATURE_CONFIGS[type].chewHits`), not on the connector. Defender toughness defined on the attacker — exactly what R72 objected to about the goblin. R75 fixes it.

### Consequences — including the costs

- `TURRET_DEFENDER_MAX_HP` and `PRINCESS_DEFENDER_MAX_HP` are **DELETED**, alongside the three goblin derivations. Same defect, same treatment.
- `Defender.hp` is a REQUIRED serialized + hashed field (`SerializedDefender.hp`, `DefenderHashed`). **Removing it is a wire removal** — clean under the 28→29 bump already being paid.
- `damageEntity`'s `'defender'` arm and `applyRadialDamage`'s defender loop **re-point at bonds**. `destroyDefender(…, 'damage')` disappears; recipe-break becomes the sole death path, as S103 originally designed.
- ⚠ **THE ONE REAL NEW COST: a REQUIRED hashed `Bond` field for accumulated damage.** Today the damage state lives on the attacker as `chewProgress` (on `CreatureHashed`). Under connector-HP it must pool on the bond — which is the ten-site / two-tsc-forced trap from the S150 recount. It also **changes behaviour for the better**: two chewers on one bond currently track separately and each must do the full work; pooled on the bond they cooperate.
- R72's *"laserTurret attacks BOTH units and towers"* now means a turret shoots the enemy tower's **connectors**. Coherent — and it makes recipe choice matter for durability. Real emergent depth, not a workaround.

## 5b. ⭐ OWNER RULING R76 — THE CONNECTOR DEFENCE FORMULA (fully determined)

> *"DEFENCE of a structure matters on the ammount of connectors or bonds. the first two shapes
> interconnected do not have any def … but only 1 HP. if there are three shapes connected in a row so
> with only two connectors the shapes overall defensive strength is 2HPx1.2DEF which makes it 2.4 and
> each of those two connectors are 1.2. now if those three shapes are connected in a triangle form
> making 3 connectors the overall shape strength will be 3hpx1.4 = 4.2 and each of those connectors
> will be 1.4. this will make people want to build complex structures with as many connectors as
> possible and not just active towers that build soldiers or shoot."*

**DEF = connectorCount − 1**, confirmed by all three owner data points (1→DEF 0, 2→DEF 1, 3→DEF 2).
Structure HP = connectorCount. Total = `connectors × (1 + 0.2 × (connectors − 1))`.
Per-connector = total ÷ connectors = **the multiplier itself** — the HP term cancels exactly.

### The entire connector-defence system is ONE exact-integer expression

```
per-connector durability, in fifths  =  connectorCount + 4
```
1→5 (×1.0) · 2→6 (×1.2) · 3→7 (×1.4) · 11→15 (×3.0). No floats anywhere; `damageEntity`'s integer
guard can never fire.

**⚠ Revision flagged to the owner:** their earlier 10-shape example gave ×3.2 and *"2 laser attacks"*.
Under the confirmed `connectors − 1` rule it is ×3.0 = 15 fifths, and a 3-ATK laser (15 fifths) destroys
a connector in **one** hit. Formula is the owner's; the outcome moved.

### Cap: NONE (owner-ruled)
> *"No cap - def climbs with the structure complexity. the reason players wont want to do only that
> though that super complex towers dont have powers … youd have to chose the tactic."*

Balance comes from **opportunity cost**, not an arithmetic ceiling: hyper-complex structures neither
shoot nor produce units, so maximal geometric defence means no army. That is the intended trade.

### Implementation consequences
- ⭐ **DEF IS DYNAMIC.** It derives from the current connector count of the connected **component**.
  Destroying a connector drops the count → drops DEF → **weakens every remaining connector**. Structures
  crumble at an *accelerating* rate. Strong game feel, but it must be named, not discovered as a bug.
- **Component-scoped, not global.** The BFS machinery already exists (`game/structure.ts`).
- **Partial-damage rule required:** store accumulated damage on the bond; compare against *current*
  capacity. A shrinking structure can make an already-damaged connector snap immediately.
- **The structure-death rule needs NO code.** `severSplit` (structure.ts:107-137) already deletes the
  smaller side on an acyclic cut and deletes nothing on a cycle cut. The owner's *"less than half (49%)"*
  **is** "the smaller side". Verified this session.
