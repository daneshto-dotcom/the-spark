## 4. How to generate in Gemini

1. Open gemini.google.com, start a new chat for every image (old chats leak the previous scene), and pick the image tool with the Pro / Thinking model (Nano Banana Pro). That is the same model family the six originals came from (gemini-3-pro-image); the fast Nano Banana model is weaker at layout instructions.
2. Optional but recommended: attach the two races' existing masters (paths on each card) as style references and add one line: "Use the attached images ONLY for palette, materials and mood - not for their camera angle." This is how the originals were kept as a family (the 2p set was seeded off the 4p set).
3. Paste the prompt. If the result is not portrait, reply: "Same image, but as a tall portrait 4:5 frame."
4. Generate 2-4 tries per pair and keep the best one. Download at full size (the download button, not a screenshot).
5. Send the file path to the session. It does the crop, resize, flip wiring, brightness check and commit.

Reject if:
- Any sky, horizon, moon or side-on view (it cannot be flipped and will not match the board).
- Bright or white patches, neon colour, or a busy, high-contrast picture.
- Any text, runes that read as letters, a watermark, a frame or a vignette.
- Characters, creatures, skeletons or standing buildings.
- A hard straight line, wall, river or road across the middle, or a two-panel look.
- Landmarks against the right edge, in the left corners, or in the bottom strip.
- Anything that looks like a known game, film or franchise.
- Landscape or square output.
