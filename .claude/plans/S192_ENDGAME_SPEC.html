3 How they come and whom they hunt

They come from the quarry centre in pulses, with each living player in turn getting the next one, so the shares stay equal. Each pants remembers its target. If that player is knocked out, the leftovers spread across the survivors by id. Everyone's towers, castle guns and units fight them, because a pants belongs to no player.
