### A5 · Chain lightning has no falloff, and one bolt cascades a whole tower

multiple connectors/targets that are within range of one another … maywe we do max6 or something"* —

and the same docblock pre-registered the trigger: *"If it plays too strong the dial is here, and
falloff is the obvious first thing to add."* He has now played it. The condition fired.
⚠ So this is NOT an ignored ruling (unlike R173). It is my provisional call, and its stated trigger.
THE CASCADE, derived from raw code: `hit = attackFifths(3,6) = 33`, computed **once** outside the
loop, applied to up to 6 links; severs are queued and dispatched *after* the loop, so the pool stays
at `structurePoolFifths(5) = 50` for all six. Banked damage carries on sever (`drain`, overkill
carries): 33 → 66 **SEVER** → 49 → 82 **SEVER** → 65 **SEVER** → 48. **Three of five connectors gone
to ONE bolt**, and the 2-connector remnant is left holding 48 against a 14 pool — already over.
FIX: per-jump integer halving, `hit_k = attackFifths(atk,pen) >> k` → 33·16·8·4·2·1 (total 64, from
198). Same tower: 33 → 49 → 57 **SEVER** → 11 → 13 → 14 ⇒ **exactly one connector per bolt**, while
the first two links still one-shot a goblin. Integer-only (no float accumulators in the sim).
⚠ THE HALVING CURVE IS MINE. Recorded at the constant with this measurement; §B-Q2 for his dial.
