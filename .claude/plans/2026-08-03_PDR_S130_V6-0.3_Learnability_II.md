## 4. RISKS

- **R-A (highest): the fix reproducing the bug it fixes.** If the two banner halves disagree about
  who owns `tierBannerFrames`, the drain can latch text that never animates, or the animate can run
  against a watermark the drain never advanced. Mitigation: field-by-field ownership pinned in the
  spec above rather than left to implementation.
- **R-B: physics-cause toast spam.** No Council leg addressed frequency. Per-frame coalescing bounds
  it to one toast per frame, but under overwrite-restart a chronically rubber-banding structure holds
  a persistent OVERSTRETCHED toast. Bounded by "however many bonds are over-tension". **First thing
  to watch in the playtest**; cheapest mitigation is dropping the physics row entirely — it is the
  one row with no attribution to make.
- **R-C: the drain-order lock protects only its allowlist.** Nothing stops a future session adding an
  unlisted `world.effects` reader after the wipe. Honest framing: *this pins today's six consumers and
  trips if any of them moves* — not *this cannot happen again*.
- **R-D: documenting the ~5/6 hole is not filling it.** The roadmap row must not read as though the
  feature works everywhere.
- **R-E: exhaustive `switch` on `cause` is a latent crash** — `'godly'` is in the effect union but not
  the action union, so an exhaustive switch either fails to compile or returns undefined. Explicit
  fallback string required.
- **R-F: three-way occlusion** (tier + combo + sever) becomes reachable for the first time *because of*
  this slot's own banner fix, and is unobserved.
