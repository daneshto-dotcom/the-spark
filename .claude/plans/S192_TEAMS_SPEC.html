2 How v1 plays

  • Default is today's game. Every seat starts on its own team — a free-for-all is byte-for-byte unchanged.
  • Pick a team by clicking your team chip. In the bot lobby every bot has one too — bots can be on your team.
  • Teammates sit side by side MINE — the host orders seats so allies share a border, never the diagonal. In a 2v2, team of the host takes the left half, the other team the right — your own v2 picture.
  • At least two teams MINE — if everyone picks the same team, Begin is disabled.
  • Win — last team standing on castles; on points, the first seat to the bar wins for its whole team. The banner says TEAM 2 WINS.
