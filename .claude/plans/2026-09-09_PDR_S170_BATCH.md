**Q-T1 RAID — YES, the enemy raids the whole building, by targeting the STRUCTURE.** Owner:
*"the enemy can still raid the whole building. He's targeting the main structure... The structure
represents the connectors building us. The foundations are the connectors. So they're attacking the

**Q-T2 — CONNECTORS STAY HIDDEN DURING BOTH BUILD AND FIGHT.** Owner: *"Connectors stay hidden during
the fight and during build. If there's a tower on top of them, you can't see the connectors, the
shapes and the primitives and the connectors between them. That's the whole idea. You can only see

They reappear ONLY on destruction.

**Q-T3 — ONE HP BAR FOR THE STRUCTURE, per-connector damage underneath.** Owner: *"there's one HP for
the whole structure. The core connector just adds to the HP pool... each one underneath merely takes
damage."*
⚠ **VERIFIED AGAINST THE TREE, AND HIS "isn't that how we defined it ten sessions ago" IS HALF RIGHT.**
There is no pooled-HP field today: bonds carry `connectorCapacityFifths(n) = n + 4` and primitives
carry 1000. So the pool is a NEW DISPLAY, not an existing mechanic. Implementing it as
**per-connector HP underneath + one aggregated bar and damage states on the tower** satisfies his
sentence exactly, changes no balance, and keeps chewers meaningful. That is the chosen route.

**Q-T4 — ONE DESTROYED CONNECTOR BREAKS THE STRUCTURE.** From Q-T1: *"once they hit it enough to
destroy at least one connector"* the tower shows damage while under attack, and on destruction the
connectors return. So: damage states while the pool drains; structure breaks when a connector dies.

## R170 — NEW OWNER BUG, VERIFIED STILL LIVE: the drone blows up harmlessly in its own zone

Owner: *"the drone sometimes still explodes in his own building or in his own area when the fight just
starts... he didn't destroy anything but I saw him blow up in my zone when the fight started."*

**VERIFIED, mechanism found, it persists.**
1. `hostTick.ts:1619` — `const fuseExpiring = world.tick >= droneCandidate.despawnAtTick - 1;` and at
   `:1644` a drone whose fuse expires dispatches `DRONE_EXPLODE` **in place, wherever it is**. The
   docblock above it names this exact failure for the goblin bomber: *"it would have flown until its
   FUSE expired and then detonated wherever it happened to be."*
2. `applyDroneExplode` severs **ENEMY bonds only**, and `applyRadialDamage` is owner-sparing — so a
   fuse-expiry blast at home can damage nothing.
3. But `droneLifecycle.ts:139` pushes `world.effects.push({ kind: 'BOMB_EXPLODE', ... })`
   **UNCONDITIONALLY**, before and regardless of whether anything was hit.
⇒ Full explosion VFX, zero effect. Most visible right after the bell, when the hub's first drone is
emitted far from any enemy and the 8 s fuse (`DRONE_LIFETIME_TICKS = 480`) runs out mid-flight. Note
the trigger only runs in `SEEKING`, so the 30-tick materialize window eats part of the fuse too.

### P2b — THE FIX (bundled with the other bug fixes)
On fuse expiry with **no enemy candidate in radius**, despawn silently instead of detonating: move the
`BOMB_EXPLODE` push to AFTER the candidate scan and gate it on something actually being severed or
damaged. Preserves "explode in place when near enemies", removes the misleading nothing-burger.
⚠ A silent fade is what the original docblock deliberately rejected — so the gate is on
"hit nothing", not on "fuse expired", which keeps its intent.
