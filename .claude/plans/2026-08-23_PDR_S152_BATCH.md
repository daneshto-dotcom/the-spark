### Challenges adopted
- **GEMINI C1 — graph-split race.** Two raids on one component in one tick could read different
  `componentConnectorCount`. Mitigation: `damageConnector` already re-reads capacity per call and
  intents are applied sequentially in one reducer thread; the sever is a dispatch, so ordering is
  deterministic. Pinned by a two-raids-one-component test.
- **GEMINI C3 — SEVER vs RAID overlap.** Resolved from the tree, not by argument: RAID *replaces* the
  direct purchase of an instant cut. SEVER_BOND still runs, as the consequence of damage reaching
  capacity. ⚠ **CAPABILITY LOSS, FLAGGED FOR OWNER PLAYTEST:** a raid (10 fifths) can never sever a
  connector in a component of ≥7 connectors (capacity = count + 4). Guaranteed cuts are gone.
- **GEMINI FM2 / accrual fractions.** "1 point per 2 towers OR 5 connections" needs a remainder rule.
  Adopted: ONE integer accumulator in tenths — tower `+5`, hand connection `+2`, threshold `10`.
  2 towers = 10 ✓, 5 connections = 10 ✓, and mixed play never wastes partial progress.
- **GEMINI FM3 / float timers.** Cloud lifetime is `3 × PHYSICS_HZ = 180` integer ticks. No `dt`.
- **GROK — bot budgets.** `botBrain` gates on `disruptionCharges` for sever/shrink; those are
  untouched, so no bot regression. Bots do not yet raid — logged as carry-forward, not silently absorbed.
- **GROK — texture census.** e2e/render-heap.spec.ts `TEXTURE_LIMIT = 8` on growth; +3 atlases against
  an already-observed +1. **Must be measured, not assumed.**
