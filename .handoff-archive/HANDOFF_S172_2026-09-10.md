### The health bar — TWO separate causes, and the second only surfaced after the first shipped
- **`7b97ba5`** — `span()`'s 9 px floor was applied to the FILL as well as the TRACK. The floor
  binds below 7.01 fifths, so **six unit types drew a bar frozen at 100 % from birth to death** —
  goblinMelee, goblinHound, goblinArcher, raceUnit, locustCloud, chewer. Above the floor the sqrt
  still under-reported: half health drew 71 %. Fill is now linear; the track keeps the sqrt, so
  R171-E's *"the big Kraken will have a big health bar"* survives untouched.
- **`7506761`** — he played it and still saw nothing. `drawHealthBars` is called from
  `GoblinRenderer` and was handed a sprite map populated behind
  `if (!GOBLIN_KINDS.has(c.type)) continue`, so **every boss, tier-3 unit, Voltkin, direwolf and
  chewer** measured `null`, fell back to `FALLBACK_SPRITE_H = 26`, and had its bar drawn INSIDE the
  body. `CreatureRenderer.spriteBoxOf()` now feeds it; `main.ts` wires them.
- **`c19e883`** — Helga, identical cause, via `PrincessRenderer`.

### Floating damage numbers — `37f376d`, `c19e883`, `ed99e51`
Kanit 900 Italic, red with a white outline, his pick. **No new synced field and no protocol bump**:
`ehp` is already on the wire, so a DROP between observations is the damage event carrying its own
value — which also covers damage-over-time for free (R171-F required it; an effects-push design
would have missed it).
- **Placement is victim-weighted**, his correction: not the midpoint but a further 50 % back toward
  the victim ⇒ `t = 0.25`. Two creatures trading blows put their numbers on opposite sides of centre.
- **The killing blow is a DISAPPEARANCE, not a delta** — the creature leaves `world.creatures` the
  same tick. The watcher carries last-seen position AND owner forward. ⚠ The owner field is
  load-bearing: without it the lookup returns `undefined`, every creature counts as an enemy, and
  the fatal number flies at the victim's own ally. Pinned with a control test.
- **Heals in green**, same face/size/geometry so they stack against hits instead of overlapping.
- Animation taken, not invented: **no library exists** (8 npm searches, awesome-pixijs, all 40
  PixiJS org repos — zero). Timings measured from MapleStory's client; pop and fling from an MIT
  WoW addon.
