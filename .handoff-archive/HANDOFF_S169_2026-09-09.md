## 5. ART AND ABILITY VFX

- ⛔ **No boss skill in this game draws anything at all.** Six bosses — the zombie rot aura, Vlad's
  life sap, RAGE, the direwolf summon, the Archdemon's take-to-hell and teleport, the Kraken's sonar
  — every one mechanically live and visually silent. The stun's "seeing stars" (S169) is the first
  derived boss-skill visual in the codebase.
- So **ability art is TWO deliverables per ability**: the VFX itself, and a renderer that DERIVES it
  from synced state. ⚠ Derive it per frame — a one-shot `world.effects` push is lost ~5/6 of the
  time, because effects sample into snapshots at 10 Hz while the renderer wipes them at 60.
- **The Archdemon "looks ridiculous... too fast" is FRAME SPARSITY, not a timing bug.** Measured:
  12 frames × 5 ticks = exactly 60 ticks, against an attack cadence of exactly 60. Bosses want MORE
  FRAMES, not a slower cadence.
- **The Kraken attack clip is edge-amputated — unrecoverable.** Those pixels were never generated;
  widening the cell just adds space around a limb that ends in a flat stump. The owner will
  regenerate from the original still. `check-clip.mjs` reports `sampleStart 60` for the pillarboxed
  half of the current clip.
- **The cheaper-generation protocol he asked for is written down — read `ART_PIPELINE.md` before any
  art work.** Its one load-bearing fact: *the pipeline does not need video, it needs 12 frames*. So:
  image-to-video seeded off his own still, ~2 s clips, and motion reused across races.
